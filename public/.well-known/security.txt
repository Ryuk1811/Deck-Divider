# Introduction
Deck Divider takes the security of our collectors and players seriously. 
We appreciate the efforts of security researchers in helping to keep our platform secure.
If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability in Deck Divider, we ask that you responsibly disclose it to us.

# Guidelines
* Please do not access or modify data belonging to other users.
* Please avoid performing actions that might degrade the performance or availability of our services (e.g., DoS, DDoS).
* Please avoid using automated scanners without our prior consent.
* Please do not exfiltrate data from our systems or exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it.

# In-Scope
* https://deckdivider.app
* All subdomains of deckdivider.app

# Out-of-Scope
* Third-party services or infrastructure (e.g., Firebase, Google Cloud, Pokemon TCG API, TCGdex, TCGplayer).
* Phishing or social engineering.
* Volumetric / Denial of Service attacks.
