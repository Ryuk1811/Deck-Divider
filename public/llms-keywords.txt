## 2. Pokémon TCG Terminology & Glossary Mappings

### ACE SPEC (Gameplay)
- **Definition**: Powerful Trainer and Special Energy cards introduced in Black & White and reintroduced in Scarlet & Violet. A standard 60-card tournament deck is permitted strictly 1 ACE SPEC card in total.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Prime Catcher, Unfair Stamp, Secret Box, Hero's Cape, Sparkling Crystal.
- **Direct App Link**: [Check ACE SPEC Rule Compliance](https://deckdivider.app/tool/deck-legality-checker)

### Secret Illustration Rare (SIR) (Rarity)
- **Definition**: Highest-tier chase card rarity in the Scarlet & Violet era, featuring full-bleed custom artwork, unique textures, and card numbers exceeding the set's standard count.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Charizard ex SIR (Obsidian Flames #223), Greninja ex SIR (Twilight Masquerade #214).
- **Direct App Link**: [Browse SIR Checklists by Set](https://deckdivider.app/sets)

### Special Art Rare (SAR) (Rarity)
- **Definition**: Japanese terminology equivalent to English Secret Illustration Rares (SIR) or Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery full arts with gold foil or distinctive textures.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Iono SAR (Clay Burst / Paldea Evolved), Mew ex SAR (Shiny Treasure ex / Paldean Fates).
- **Direct App Link**: [View Japanese & English Equivalents](https://deckdivider.app/sets)

### Illustration Rare (IR) (Rarity)
- **Definition**: Single-star rarity cards in Scarlet & Violet featuring scenic, full-card illustrations of standard non-ex Pokémon (historically known as Art Rares or Character Rares).
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Charmander IR (151 #168), Cleffa IR (Obsidian Flames #202), Magikarp IR (Paldea Evolved #203).
- **Direct App Link**: [Track IR Binder Checklists](https://deckdivider.app/sets)

### Masterball Reverse Holo (Rarity)
- **Definition**: Special reverse holographic foil pattern exclusive to Pokémon Card 151 and Prismatic Evolutions featuring repeated Master Ball stamps across the card background (1 per booster box in Japanese 151).
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Gengar Masterball Holo, Pikachu Masterball Holo, Erika's Invitation Masterball Holo.
- **Direct App Link**: [Open 151 Masterball Checklist](https://deckdivider.app/set/sv3pt5)

### PSA 10 Gem Mint (Grading)
- **Definition**: Professional Sports Authenticator (PSA) highest standard grade, indicating virtually perfect 55/45 to 60/40 centering, zero print lines, flawless corners, pristine edges, and untouched surface gloss.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: A PSA 10 modern alternate art typically commands a 2.5x to 4.5x market premium over its raw Near Mint counterpart.
- **Direct App Link**: [Calculate PSA 10 Price Spread](https://deckdivider.app/tool/psa-grade-calculator)

### BGS Black Label 10 (Grading)
- **Definition**: Beckett Grading Services (BGS) holy grail certification awarded only when all four individual subgrades (Centering, Corners, Edges, Surface) receive a perfect 10.0 score.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: BGS Black Label modern cards frequently trade for 5x to 15x standard PSA 10 values at major auction houses.
- **Direct App Link**: [Explore BGS Multiplier Modeling](https://deckdivider.app/tool/psa-grade-calculator)

### Regulation Mark (Gameplay)
- **Definition**: A letter printed on the bottom-left corner of modern Pokémon cards (e.g., F, G, H, I) determining tournament format legality. Rotations periodically retire older letters from Standard format play.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Standard format in 2025-2026 permits regulation marks G, H, and I.
- **Direct App Link**: [View Current Standard Format Rules](https://deckdivider.app/format/standard)

### PTCGL Deck String (Gameplay)
- **Definition**: The official standardized plaintext formatting syntax used by Pokémon TCG Live and tournament organizers to import and export 60-card deck lists in one click.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Pokémon: 14 / Trainer: 34 / Energy: 12 formatting structure.
- **Direct App Link**: [Copy PTCGL String for Meta Decks](https://deckdivider.app/deck/charizard-ex)

### Near Mint (NM) (Condition)
- **Definition**: Card condition standard showing minimal to no wear from handling, with clean borders, no whitening over 1mm, no creases, scratches, or surface indentations.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Baseline condition for raw single card pricing across TCGplayer and Cardmarket.
- **Direct App Link**: [Track Raw NM Market Values](https://deckdivider.app/pricing)

### Elite Trainer Box (ETB) (Product)
- **Definition**: A flagship collector product containing 9 booster packs (11 in special sets like 151 and Crown Zenith), exclusive foil promo card, card sleeves, dice, and energy cards.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Prismatic Evolutions ETB, 151 ETB with Snorlax Illustration Rare promo.
- **Direct App Link**: [Browse Sets with ETB Releases](https://deckdivider.app/sets)

### Lost Zone (Gameplay)
- **Definition**: An out-of-play game area distinct from the discard pile. Cards sent to the Lost Zone cannot be recovered by any card effects, powering mechanics like Comfey, Sableye, and Mirage Gate.
- **Representative Cards/Mechanics**: Lost Zone Box archetype, Giratina VSTAR Lost Impact.
- **Direct App Link**: [View Lost Zone Deck Architecture](https://deckdivider.app/deck/giratina-vstar)
